# Dragon Ball Daima is a 20‑episode anime that follows the adventures of Son Goku, who, along with his friends, is turned into children by a wish from the new Supreme King of the Demon Realm, Gomah. The story takes place after the defeat of Kid Buu, Goku and his allies must journey through the Demon Realm to reverse the effects of Gomah's wish and rescue Dende, the Demon Realm's Dragon
